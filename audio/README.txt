Original soundtrack for 層渡りの旅録 (v3.1.0)

Five campaign themes:
- tutorial-theme.mp3: welcoming and gentle, 84 BPM; bright flute, harp, warm pad harmony and light percussion.
- beginner-theme.mp3: moss-covered ruins, 92 BPM; exploratory D-minor/modal harmony, flute, harp and strings.
- intermediate-theme.mp3: blue crystal sanctuary, 100 BPM; luminous piano lead, crystalline pulse, soft pads and melodic harp.
- advanced-theme.mp3: ash cavern, 108 BPM; urgent brass lead, repeated piano/strings, heavy frame drums and low bass.
- expert-theme.mp3: star-devouring abyss, 116 BPM; dark chromatic harmony, relentless string ostinato, brass, deep bass and heavy percussion.
- boss-theme.mp3: shared boss battle theme; forceful low-string ostinato, brass melody, bass and cinematic percussion.

All six tracks are original compositions synthesized locally for this project. No third-party samples, loops, recordings or external music services are used. All tracks are bundled in the Service Worker shell for offline play after the first successful load.
